The strokes

The tonbak's voice comes from a goat-skin head over a hollow mulberry body that narrows to an open bell. Where you strike it decides which system rings: the centre drives the air cavity and gives the deep Tom, the rim barely couples to it at all and gives the dry Bak. Every stroke below is modelled on that behaviour rather than sampled, so it responds to velocity and never repeats itself exactly.
